- **Cinema & Writing:** Festival de Cannes Official Selection ([*Afterthought / Hayored Lema'ala*](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/hayored-lema-ala/)) & Cinéfondation 1st Prize Winner ([*Himnon / Anthem*](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/himnon/)); Librettist of contemporary opera [*When he was good*](https://www.freshsqueezedopera.com/whenhewasgood) (selected for production by Fresh Squeezed Opera, NYC).
